# Stress

Stress is a common response to situations that feel demanding, uncertain, or difficult to manage.

People may experience stress in different ways. Common experiences can include feeling tense, overwhelmed, restless, having difficulty concentrating, or feeling physically tired.

Some practical strategies that may help with everyday stress include:

- Taking a short break from the situation.
- Breaking a large task into smaller, manageable steps.
- Getting some gentle physical activity such as walking.
- Practicing slow, comfortable breathing.
- Maintaining a regular sleep schedule.
- Talking with someone you trust about what you are experiencing.
- Making time for activities that help you relax.

It can also be useful to identify situations that repeatedly contribute to stress and consider whether any part of the situation can be changed or approached differently.

If stress is persistent, significantly affecting daily life, or becoming difficult to manage, speaking with a qualified mental health professional can provide additional support.

This information is general educational information and is not a diagnosis or a substitute for professional medical care.
