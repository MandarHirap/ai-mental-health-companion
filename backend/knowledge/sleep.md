# Sleep

Sleep is an important part of physical and emotional wellbeing. Difficulties with sleep can sometimes occur during periods of stress or emotional difficulty.

Helpful sleep habits can include:

- Keeping a relatively consistent sleep and wake schedule.
- Creating a quiet and comfortable sleep environment.
- Giving yourself time to wind down before going to bed.
- Limiting stimulating activities close to bedtime.
- Getting regular physical activity during the day.
- Using the bed primarily for sleep when possible.

If your mind is busy at night, writing down thoughts or tasks before bed may help create a clearer transition into rest.

Occasional difficulty sleeping can happen to many people. If sleep problems continue, become severe, or significantly affect daytime functioning, speaking with a qualified healthcare professional can provide additional guidance.
