# Grounding Techniques

Grounding techniques can help someone redirect attention toward the present moment when they feel overwhelmed, anxious, or emotionally unsettled.

One simple approach is to notice things around you using your senses. For example, you can identify things you can see, hear, feel, smell, or taste.

Other grounding approaches include:

- Taking slow, comfortable breaths.
- Placing your feet firmly on the floor and noticing the physical sensation.
- Describing objects around you in simple detail.
- Naming the current date, location, and what you are doing.
- Holding a cool or comfortable object and paying attention to how it feels.
- Taking a slow walk while paying attention to your surroundings.

Grounding is not a replacement for professional treatment, but it can be a simple way to bring attention back to the present moment.

If distress is persistent or difficult to manage, consider reaching out to someone you trust or a qualified mental health professional.
