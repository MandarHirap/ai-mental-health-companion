# Anxiety

Anxiety can involve feelings of worry, nervousness, uneasiness, or fear about situations that feel uncertain or difficult.

People can experience anxiety in different ways. Some may notice racing thoughts, difficulty concentrating, restlessness, muscle tension, or changes in sleep.

Some practical approaches that may help with everyday anxiety include:

- Taking slow, comfortable breaths and giving yourself a moment to pause.
- Focusing attention on what is happening in the present moment.
- Breaking stressful tasks into smaller steps.
- Reducing avoidable sources of stress when possible.
- Maintaining regular sleep, meals, and physical activity.
- Talking with someone you trust about what you are experiencing.
- Writing down worries and identifying which parts you can realistically act on.

It can be helpful to remember that experiencing anxiety does not by itself mean that someone has an anxiety disorder.

If anxiety is persistent, becoming difficult to manage, or significantly interfering with everyday life, speaking with a qualified mental health professional can provide additional support.
