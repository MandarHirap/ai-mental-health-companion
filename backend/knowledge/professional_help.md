# Professional Mental Health Support

A qualified mental health professional can provide individualized support for emotional or psychological difficulties.

Professional support may be useful when distress is persistent, significantly affects everyday activities, interferes with relationships or work, or becomes difficult to manage with personal coping strategies.

Depending on the situation, people may seek support from professionals such as psychologists, psychiatrists, counselors, therapists, or other qualified healthcare providers.

When seeking help, it can be useful to describe what you have been experiencing, how long it has been happening, and how it is affecting your daily life.

An AI companion can provide general information and supportive conversation, but it cannot replace individualized assessment, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency care from qualified professionals.

If someone is in immediate danger or experiencing an emergency, they should seek immediate help from local emergency services or the nearest emergency department.

This information is general educational information and is not a diagnosis or a substitute for professional medical care.\n
